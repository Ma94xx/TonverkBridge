Tonverk Bridge streams your Android phone's media audio (YouTube, browser, games, music apps) to the Elektron Tonverk over USB as a 48 kHz / 24-bit class-compliant audio stream — no root required.

How it works:

* The app captures the phone's media audio via Android's AudioPlaybackCapture API (the system "start recording/casting" dialog).
* A built-in userspace UAC2 driver (libusb) sends the audio to the Tonverk's isochronous USB audio interface, with asynchronous feedback-driven rate matching and high-quality windowed-sinc resampling.
* Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere — audio goes straight from the mixer to your Tonverk.

Permissions:

* Record audio + MediaProjection: required by Android to capture the phone's own media sound.
* Notifications: shows the ongoing notification while the bridge is running.
* USB: to access the Tonverk's USB audio interface.

Limitations:

* Apps that opt out of audio capture (e.g. Spotify) stay silent by design.
* Runs only while the foreground service is active.

This is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Elektron.
