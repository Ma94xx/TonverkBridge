Tonverk Bridge streams your Android phone's media audio (YouTube, browser, games, music apps) over USB to the Elektron Tonverk — or any other class-compliant USB audio device (UAC1/UAC2) — no root required. It can also record the device's USB audio output to a WAV file on the phone.

How it works:

* The app captures the phone's media audio via Android's AudioPlaybackCapture API (the system "start recording/casting" dialog).
* A built-in userspace UAC1/UAC2 driver (libusb) sends the audio to the device's isochronous USB audio interface, with asynchronous feedback-driven rate matching and high-quality windowed-sinc resampling.
* The recording function captures the device's USB output bit-exact (WAV in the device's native format, clocked by the device) — it works with or without the bridge running.
* Nothing is stored or sent anywhere except the recordings you explicitly start; those go to Music/Tonverk Bridge or a folder you pick.

Permissions:

* Record audio + MediaProjection: required by Android to capture the phone's own media sound.
* Notifications: shows the ongoing notification while the bridge is running.
* USB: to access the device's USB audio interface.

Limitations:

* Apps that opt out of audio capture (e.g. Spotify) stay silent by design.
* Runs only while the foreground service is active.

This is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Elektron.
